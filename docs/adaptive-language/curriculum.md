## Curriculum quality gates

Each unit has an objective, year reference, prerequisites, hints and worked explanation, plus at least four initial examples. The content generator may shuffle **approved choices** and vary local complexity but cannot invent unchecked phonics facts or pass a language-model guess as a correct answer. Before expansion, educator reviewers must verify that grade alignment, language patterns, passage complexity, plausible distractors, all permitted answers and explanations are accurate and developmentally appropriate. Every scored question should be unambiguous; creative-writing questions remain explicitly unscored.

The initial bank is breadth-first and does not replicate the thousands of vendor-defined exercises in IXL or the structured decoding and fluency progression of Lexia. No one should substitute it for comprehensive reading instruction. Expand to additional validated examples, explicit sound-based instruction and varied passages only after checking observed learning evidence and item performance.

The [early crosswalk](../adaptive-math/EARLY-CROSSWALK.md) lists the Pre-K and Kindergarten units. The v2 catalog adds early learning to the historical v1 pack. The v3 workspace pack adds 52 examples across ten skills. Two Kindergarten skills cover ending sounds and sound segmentation. Grade 1 plural spelling and Grade 2 sentence construction include repeated pieces. Grade 2 also adds vowel-team gaps and adjective selection. Grade 3 adds subject–verb gap completion and selectable evidence. Grade 4 adds evidence-based inference and short unscored writing about a visible passage. The English integration contract documents how these responses reach the subject engine. Items retain their pack identity on export and import.
