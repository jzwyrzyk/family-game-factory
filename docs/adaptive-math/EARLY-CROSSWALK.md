## Reference editions

Michigan's Early Childhood Standards of Quality for Birth to Kindergarten were approved November 15, 2022. The early tasks contribute to the mathematics and communication/language/literacy strands. Row-level preschool codes above are the Head Start ELOF preschool goals from the federal Ages Birth to Five framework. Kindergarten codes refer to Common Core. The local pattern extension has no claimed kindergarten Common Core pattern standard. Numeral recognition only covers the recognition portion of writing-number objectives. Printed word choices sample recognition. They do not measure reading fluency.

- [Michigan early childhood standards](https://www.michigan.gov/mileap/early-childhood-education/early-learners-and-care/gsrp/early-childhood-standards-of-quality)
- [Federal Head Start Early Learning Outcomes Framework](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-HE23_1100-PURL-gpo65456/pdf/GOVPUB-HE23_1100-PURL-gpo65456.pdf)
- [Kindergarten mathematics](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/K/introduction/) and [counting and cardinality](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/K/CC/)
- [Kindergarten foundational reading](https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/RF/K/)

The counting and construction tasks accept taps. Numeral identification uses spoken number names. Concrete changes to collections and within-ten operations use separate skill records. Listening tasks record responses under listening, vocabulary or sound awareness. A written stimulus used for recognition stays silent. Sound matching uses spoken whole-word anchors. Raw consonant spellings are never passed to speech as isolated phonemes.

All pictured content is served from the application origin. Candidate food, animal and familiar-object illustrations are locally rasterized Noto artwork with notices in public/early-pictures. Owner artwork and curriculum review are pending. New content has no educator or child efficacy validation.

Story sequencing also uses the host’s tile evaluator. The learner taps two or three event pictures in narrated order, can undo any choice, and submits the completed sequence. This records listening evidence.
