# Brio artwork adoption and form-specific expression review

**Status:** Accepted for Brio's exact Base and corrected Grown neutral artwork; six new expressions are in review.
**Recorded:** November 19, 2026 (America/New_York).
**Decision source:** The owner selected the first (Base) and corrected third (Grown) images in the Brio review, then requested emotions and inclusion in the existing shared artwork PR. This record paraphrases the decision without publishing the private conversation or source photograph.

## Context

A guitar-inspired exploration first produced Timbri. The owner requested another attempt. The replacement Brio direction is a crystal-backed hopper whose paired sound anatomy grows from compact solid pick-shaped plates into swept crystal-framed resonance sails. The first Brio Grown attempt included an extra raised appendage; that defect was repaired before selection.

## Decision

- Add Brio to the established [character-reference index](../pals/art/character-references.json), with the unique design reference `GP-S025` and character key `brio-2026-11-19/brio`. These are design identifiers, not application IDs. No historical board letter is assigned.
- Preserve the exact selected compact [Base](../pals/art/characters/brio/initial.png) and corrected [Grown](../pals/art/characters/brio/growth.png) PNG bytes. They are independently approved masters in `forms.base` and `forms.mature`.
- Maintain exactly two total forms and one transition. Preserve six legs, paired amber gem anatomy, two antennae, the cinnamon/cream palette and face construction in future edits.
- Develop Celebrate, Thinking and Encouraging retry for each form, always using that form's approved neutral master. Publish the six resulting images to the shared artwork PR as **in-review candidates**; do not infer approval from the generation request or internal QA.
- Keep one current image per form/state. Bundle two masters and six expression candidates; omit rejected alternatives, the uncorrected extra-limb attempt and duplicate preview rasters.
- Preserve all previous character entries and their source metadata. The other profiles' imported status records remain dated evidence; this scoped addition does not re-audit their later approvals.

## Consequences and limits

The represented index increases from 22 to **23 roster entries**, and from 23 to **24 individual profiles**, because the pre-existing Navi/Mote entry still contains two profiles. Those counts describe this index, not a claim that the complete external master collection or expression work has been synchronized.

Brio's [visual profile and emotion review](../pals/art/characters/brio/README.md) presents the eight current images, purpose, family anchors, growth and internal QA. The authoritative index records exact hashes and the separate neutral/expression decisions.

These files retain ivory presentation backgrounds. The change does not create isolated sprites, a rig, an animation set, runtime mappings, acquisition prices, progression thresholds or deployment. Existing saved collections, games and backups are unchanged. The separate companion implementation and two-form runtime reconciliation remain outside this art-only addition.

## Evidence

- Exact original neutral and expression PNG bytes retained and hashed during import.
- Actual pixels checked against each form's approved master; all six candidates preserve six legs, two antennae, paired gem roots, materials and readable emotion differences.
- The corrected grown anatomy is the only included Grown neutral.
- Same shared [artwork PR #74](https://github.com/jzwyrzyk/family-game-factory/pull/74), with the [Gem Pal Studio](../pals/STUDIO.md) method and [continuity rules](../pals/CONTINUITY.md).
