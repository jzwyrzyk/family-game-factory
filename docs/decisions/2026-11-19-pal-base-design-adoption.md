# Approved Gem Pal base-design adoption

**Status:** Accepted for the six displayed base designs. The historical growth pause below is superseded by the later [form-selection decision](2026-11-19-pal-form-reconciliation.md); the original base approval remains in effect.
**Recorded:** November 19, 2026 (America/New_York).
**Decision source/date:** The owner's November 19, 2026 art-review follow-up approved S, T, U, V, W and X for adoption and explicitly asked to wait on growth while that approach changes in another conversation. This record paraphrases that direction; it does not reproduce a private transcript or infer the other conversation's replacement design.

## Context

The design work has sixteen previously selected roster entries and six newly reviewed base concepts. The separate [companion implementation PR68](https://github.com/jzwyrzyk/family-game-factory/pull/68) uses three persistent provisional identities. The concept boards and design-catalog IDs do not establish a mapping to those runtime identities, saved collection records or scene assets.

## Decision

Adopt **S Poff (`GP-S019`), T Chimi (`GP-S020`), U Pleeko (`GP-S021`), V Talli (`GP-S022`), W Zippa (`GP-S023`) and X Whorl (`GP-S024`)** at approved board revision 2. Preserve their base specifications, protected identity anchors and exact board bytes in the [approved-base registry](../pals/art/README.md).

Carry forward A, B, D, E, F, G, H, I, K, L, M, N, O, P, Q and R as prior selection summaries. The selected roster now contains **22 entries**; C and J remain set aside. The earlier sixteen summaries are not substitutes for their complete original artwork records or canonical identity registry.

Pause growth work. This decision approves no growth forms or replacement growth approach. It leaves expression sets, animation rigs, isolated production assets, runtime identity binding and acquisition policy pending, with no invented thresholds or mappings.

## Consequences and limits

The project gains an inspectable record of the approved base designs and their provenance without changing application behavior or saved identity. The two multi-character boards are reference artwork; individual game assets require separate preparation. Design affordances and proposed actions are fictional briefs, not evidence of implemented or physically validated mechanics.

The implementation handoff must reconcile the selected roster with persistent runtime and scene IDs. Base approval does not authorize remapping an existing Pal by resemblance or name. Any later growth decision should explicitly link this pause and record the replacement approach when it is established. Existing historical implementation decisions retain their original scope.
