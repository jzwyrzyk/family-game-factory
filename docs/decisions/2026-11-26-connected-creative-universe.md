# Preserve the child's original artwork across 2D activities

The product owner's Connected Creative Universe directive establishes permanent authorship and reuse rules. A child keeps their drawing, chooses its movement and gives it different jobs in different adventures. Jems may produce illustrated settings and objects and show them alongside the child's work.

The existing private drawing and scene authorities already preserve owner identity, immutable image or rig references and recoverable history. Extend these contracts across My Art, Creative Studio and trusted game hosts. Preserve exact source bytes. Assign behavior at the usage site and pin every kept use. Separate prepared representations from originals. New games use 2D rendering and compatible movement systems. Preserve retained games and existing learning, reward and consent contracts.

This decision excludes generative transformation of children's artwork permanently. General private-asset delivery to generated code requires independent security and release qualification. The owner authorized development of A–F and original Jems product art. Artwork acceptance, hosted acceptance, production promotion and external activation each require their own evidence.
