# Unified Math and English learning workspace

**Decision date** November 26, 2026. The product owner approved the comprehensive learning interface investigation and requested implementation across Pre-K through Grade 4. This decision records the shared product and engineering direction. Implementation and acceptance evidence belongs in the delivery record.

## Context

Math and English already share a trusted host, grade limits and pause/continuation policy. Their screens used independent layouts and themes. A general numeric response could offer place-value controls even when another representation better fit the skill. Tile activities allowed assembly, while passages and grammar questions frequently used generic choices. Earlier approval of readability and narration addressed the contrast and size of the existing surfaces.

## Decision

Both subjects use the same child-facing presentation rules and profile bands. Each subject keeps its reviewed item bank, evaluator, adaptive selection and assistance rules. Define each tool's input, output, knowledge requirements and effect on evidence. Learners manipulate the activity's normal representation, explicitly request support, review a construction and return through the original host. Use registered assets and transient local work. Host policy remains authoritative for all game entrances.

## Consequences

A shared interaction can serve several curricula safely only when its defaults, bounds, accessible labels and narration suit every mapped item. Each extension requires scoring and assistance checks at the real entry point, rendered viewport evidence and a reviewed capability entry. Older records retain the original item definitions, forms and history. Product direction alone does not establish educational efficacy, device acceptance or an authorized deployment.
