# Creative Asset & World Design Standard

## The artist keeps authorship

A child's drawing is finished artwork. Keep its exact original bytes, a recoverable original, and the editable source when one exists. A crayon rock belongs in a professionally illustrated world exactly as its maker drew it. Jems must never send private artwork to a generative image model, redraw it, beautify it, adapt its appearance automatically, or attribute supplied art to the child. This doctrine applies permanently to every integration, including premium generation. Name bundled Jems stamps and environments as supplied assets.

The child may crop, rotate, flip, scale, make a transparent cutout, remove a background or use bounded compression. Any future edge smoothing requires an explicit conservative and reversible choice. Preserve the source of every preparation step. Keep the submitted bytes of an admitted original inside its existing owner and consent boundary. On a reviewed-only entry, prepare a privacy-safe file before admission and obtain approval for the exact file that will be retained. An older flattened image can still be used as a picture.

## One drawing across activities

| Concept | Example | Persistence rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owned asset | My Rock | Stable within a profile and its family workspace. Derive identity from existing private records. |
| Subject and collection | Rock, Thing | Optional descriptive input after drawing. Accept imaginative subjects and unnamed drawings. |
| Revision | Original or edited picture | Each explicit Keep creates an immutable version with a content hash and a verifiable parent when derived. |
| Representation | Editable drawing, transparent sprite, background, or motion design | Record its type and exact picture or movement revision. A render or thumbnail never replaces its source. |
| Placement | Decoration in one scene, collectible in a game | The saved project owns placement, framing, role and behavior. Only explicit game or scene rules confer mechanics. |
| Recovery | Archived rock in a saved adventure | Hide archived pictures from new selections. Retain pinned pictures for playback, export and restore. |

A picker resolves the active owner and selected version inside the trusted host. Saving an edit to My Rock never advances a saved project to the latest revision. Replacing an instance is a child choice that creates a new project version. Verify the source hash, all referenced ancestors and the owner again after asynchronous reads. A missing picture has a recovery state. Stock art cannot impersonate it.

## Drawing and movement

The canvas and immediate palette get first claim on space. Use named, recognizable tools and touch-sized controls beside the canvas or in a phone tray. Start younger artists with a short set and reveal more tools on demand. Offer keyboard drawing, equivalent accessible controls for transforms, predictable pointer cancellation, undo and redo. A failed Keep leaves the work available to retry.

Keep a bounded, versioned record of the child's operations or layers, paper transparency and the exact finished raster. Reopening a flattened historical drawing must never invent strokes. Separate reusable pointer input, editing history and rendering from profile storage. The Unified Learning Workspace may share appropriate drawing primitives. Learning scratch work stays transient and cannot automatically become permanent art, an achievement or independent learning evidence.

The child can choose a whole sprite, outline a part, design a path, or edit an existing rig. Keep movement parameters, parent relationships, attachment points and the exact picture reference editable. A path describes motion. A physics adapter needs its own explicit shape, joints and verified mechanic. A character's name and collection provide no such instruction.

## World and game construction

Build new games in 2D with transparent sprites, illustrated environment layers and compatible movement and physics systems. Depth may come from composition, light, shadows and parallax. Preserve retained games and their existing runtimes. An idea requiring unsupported mechanics returns to the child for a decision.

Use original Jems artwork at the scale and aspect ratio of actual play. Preserve approved makers, Pals, earned forms and the existing visual language of Gem Factory, Mind Your Jems! and My Art. Pin the selected environment, its framing and the placed artwork in the scene revision. Let the child choose their own drawing as a world where supported. A visual backdrop, including a hand-drawn rock, has no implicit collider.

## Trust, storage and qualification

Use the established private art, rig, scene and recovery authorities. Save and capacity-check related bytes atomically. Keep owner and consent checks on reads, writes, transfer, profile switches and delayed callbacks. Preserve existing histories, device scope, offline entry, family privacy, rewards and parental reporting. Record archive, erasure, missing-reference and recovery behavior for each new adapter.

General generated-game use requires an independently reviewed host delivery contract. Bind a selected artifact, exact revision and authorized slot. Supply only bounded raster pixels needed for that use. Strip metadata. Keep editable sources, hashes that identify private originals, arbitrary URLs, owner identifiers, credentials and stores out of generated code and model requests. End access when the session ends or its authority changes. Production admission, hosting, paid operations and family activation have separate release gates.

## Module owners and acceptance

| Workstream | Source and migration responsibility | Review evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A · Drawing Workspace | My Art owns input, document encoding and original preservation. Family entry owns privacy-safe admission. | Tool gestures, alpha export, editing after reload, older pins, failed saves, device sizes and offline sandbox. |
| B · Illustrated Worlds | Scene renderer owns registered art and framing. | Inspect the actual activity at desktop and touch sizes. |
| C · Draw-to-Animation | Motion editor owns rig/path versioning and mapping to explicit scene actions. | Save a path, replay it and reopen it for editing. |
| D · Asset Library | Private-art authority owns identity, version selection, archive and transfer. | Search, role changes, cross-profile isolation and reference integrity. |
| E · Game Integration | Trusted preview host owns scoped delivery. Generation owners maintain the 2D contract and independent security/release gate. | Synthetic artifact binding, revocation, unavailable assets and isolation. |
| F · World Building | Scene persistence owns custom background references and revision migration. | Old scene playback, selectable backgrounds, editing, export and recovery. |

Each adapter declares its source authority, supported representations, version compatibility and deletion behavior. Exercise the real entry point with synthetic art. Record exact source heads, rendered evidence, test scope and open limitations in the PR. A local screenshot cannot establish real-device, child, hosted or production acceptance.
