## Owner evidence required before real participation

- Identify the operator, adult contact channel, hosting regions, processors, subprocessors, endpoints, notice version, complaint channel, retention periods and named owners. Review content for accessibility and deliver the direct notice before collection.
- Have counsel assess the applicable age, jurisdiction, consent method, notice contents, separate disclosure choices and the parent's access, withdrawal and deletion rights. A private invitation, contact pairing, account credential and play checkbox serve different purposes. Choose an independently reviewed process and store a minimal receipt. Avoid collecting ID documents by default. [FTC COPPA FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions) · [16 CFR Part 312](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312)
- For each OpenAI organization/project, endpoint, model and data type, obtain applicable approval and evidence that ZDR is enabled where required. Check any endpoint exceptions and application-state retention. Child personal data below 13 or the applicable age of digital consent requires the provider's under-18 controls. A local flag or a request with `store:false` cannot establish this. Review safeguards, age-appropriate behavior, moderation, incident escalation and provider contract evidence independently. [Under-18 guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/safety-checks/under-18-api-guidance) · [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data)
- Confirm exact Google Cloud Speech and any TTS project, region, logging and terms. Validate the private coach/worker identity, redaction, output limits, safe generated runtime and provider disable switches. Record a scope-specific privacy digest, safety digest, notice version and expiry in private deployment configuration.
- Exercise requests with two synthetic families and the mapped household. Inspect browser caches, secret-free logs, backup recipients, soft-deleted objects and a real restoration/deletion rehearsal. Agree how long disconnected tablets may retain local records. Keep actual case evidence in the approved private system.
- For a proposed material change, pause affected capabilities, version the direct notice, inform the adult, and obtain fresh verified scopes where required. Require a new play acknowledgment. Record the exact configuration and approvals before resuming.

The verified parent export includes the family workspace, guardian participation, device metadata, consent versions, usage and acknowledgment receipts. Short-lived coach replies still held at export time are included in that family's release. The guardian downloads local data from each device. The operator dashboard receives only pending work references, stages and bounded spending amounts.

Beta Parent Controls also retain each anonymous profile's selected grade and version as minimal authorization metadata. This setting does not upload learning answers, history, games or art. An explicitly enabled supervised early-creation session records a random proof digest, learner/device/guardian bindings, current notice and verified-choice identity, grade version and short expiry. Only the tab holds the raw proof. Job exports retain learner and supervision provenance; no supervision proof is exported. Renewed notices revoke the earlier optional choices and active work while preserving original identity and saved records. These source controls do not establish independent guardian verification, legal approval or provider readiness.
