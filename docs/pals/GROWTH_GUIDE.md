# Gem Pal growth guide

**Current art policy: two total forms, Base → Grown, one transition.** This does not set reward thresholds or change saved progress. Preserve the existing art-index keys `forms.base` and `forms.mature`; **Grown** is the display name of `mature`, not a third form or a schema migration. See [Studio](STUDIO.md) for the separate artwork and implementation workstreams.

## The growth contract

Use two total forms and one transition. Before rendering, fill the growth contract:

| Question | Required answer |
| --- | --- |
| What remains? | Specific family anchors |
| What changes? | Observable architecture, proportions, stance, articulation, negative space, or locomotion |
| How do gems develop? | A more capable or integrated use of the original gem premise |
| What can it do now? | A capability or role visibly supported by anatomy |
| Why this character? | Connection to the first form's purpose, temperament, or limitation |
| What is excluded? | Relevant rejected path or meaningless escalation |

Choose a species-specific transformation: compact body to articulated climber; short structure to open arch; limited mechanism to greater range; gem manipulator to deliberate caster; maker to user of what it has made. These are options, not a mandatory sequence or a license for unrelated anatomy.

Apply these character-specific design lessons after confirming the current selected reference. These examples are not a live approval table:

- **Sivo:** Change architecture and overall feel. The river-arch direction illustrates structural change; do not simply scale up the body.
- **Weft:** Let its weave become useful. The recorded bridge direction develops the premise. Keep one character per profile and resolve its arms clearly.
- **Navi / F1:** Preserve the hovering gem relationship. The mage/caster direction follows it; the superseded Groundwarden does not.
- **Clasp:** Preserve the selected formidable Type A direction when it is the target. Different proportions and purposeful layered complexity can coexist with a friendly expression.
- **Pleeko:** Preserve the selected natural articulated climber direction when it is the target. Structural novelty must not produce awkward anatomy.
- **Talli and Whorl:** Develop extension and rotation respectively; avoid unrelated new powers.

Compare the pair at equal display height with similar framing. A larger crop can fake growth; a different pose can conceal an unchanged body. Inspect family recognition and structural difference independently.

Mentally remove glow, decorative jewelry, props, and background spectacle while retaining structural crystals. Removing anatomical gems may erase the mechanism being assessed. The remaining body-and-gem structure should still show transformation. Make the change coherent, not merely drastic.

Generate separate neutral references. Approving Grown does not replace Base. Give Grown its own anatomy, anchors, expression references, and compatibility checks.

## Repeat a controlled process

Use a small active queue: source cards → brief selection → exact-reference setup → single-form art → internal visual review → owner decision → record update. Parallelize independent resolved characters; serialize identity decisions and shared-record changes. Do not have simultaneous tasks edit the same image target.

Eliminate weak concepts before rendering. Write an exact delta before revising. Repair isolated defects without reopening approved design. Validate new architecture before making many expressions. Avoid bundling reference reconciliation, species redesign, full expression production, and runtime deployment into one unreviewable change.

Preserve the recipe, actual assets, selected references, prompts, available generator metadata, and decisions. The process can be repeatable; identical regeneration from a text prompt is not assured. Use the actual selected image to preserve identity.

## Approval boundary

Use [Quality gates](QUALITY_GATES.md) before owner review. Preserve the approved Base while reviewing Grown separately. Keep selected direction, exact art, expression, animation, integration, and in-game acceptance as separate records under [Continuity](CONTINUITY.md). A third form requires a later explicit product decision; old three-stage code does not create that decision.
