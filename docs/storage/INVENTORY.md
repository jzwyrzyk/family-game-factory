### Migration and isolation rules for every domain

- Family profile IDs on beta origins are issued by the server. Browser `familyId`, grade, parent unlock, local migration marker, backup manifest or recovery receipt never grants a remote capability. Pre-K (-1) and Kindergarten (0) are valid retained grades alongside 1–4, while the existing early-preview activation gate still governs use/import.
- Native mechanics/configuration and coding scenes retain immutable revision IDs and saved engine versions; metadata/index projections are rebuildable. Their historical records, original art and referenced rig versions are authoritative. Unknown/corrupt versions remain preserved and block replacement.
- Math, English, computational and problem-solving records retain original source/support provenance. Assisted listening/coding/parent-guided responses do not become independent mastery during migration, export, restore or synchronization.
- Adult Private uses its separately authorized audience and namespace throughout records, payload hashes, outbox, recovery points and exports. An owner pilot permission is not a family capability. Origin-scoped storage is isolation between families, not encryption against an authorized device user.
- Store preparation is opt-in and must publish a verified completion marker only after its records/dependencies are durable. Until a domain is fully integrated, the existing legacy reader/writer remains authoritative; dual-write projections must not be described as a completed authority migration. Legacy originals are retained, and rollback requires a reader that understands every written format.
- Local-only domains create no remote content merely through beta enrollment, participation, a grade change or device pairing. Remote continuity and backup need the independently approved, current domain-specific policy and device/Adult authorization. Tombstones retain conflict and recovery history; they do not authorize physical asset deletion or invent retention deadlines.
